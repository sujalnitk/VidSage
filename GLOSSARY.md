# VidSage

VidSage helps viewers discuss a captioned video and verify answers against its spoken content.

## Language

**Video conversation**:
A sequence of viewer messages and VidSage responses about one video.
_Avoid_: Cross-video chat

**Follow-up**:
A viewer message whose meaning or requested explanation depends on an earlier message or response in the same **Video conversation**.

**Video exchange**:
A viewer message and its completed VidSage response within a **Video conversation**.

**Caption evidence**:
Passages from a video's captions that support factual claims in a response.
Earlier responses are conversational context, not **Caption evidence**.

**Structured answer**:
A VidSage response with an outcome and claims whose supporting passages are drawn from **Caption evidence**.
